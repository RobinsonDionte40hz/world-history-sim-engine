# Product Overview

## World History Simulation Engine

A sophisticated turn-based simulation platform for creating dynamic historical worlds with emergent storytelling, featuring complete creative freedom through mapless, free-form world building.

## Product Vision

Create a platform where users can build and simulate rich historical worlds without constraints, watching as their creations develop organic histories through the interactions of conscious NPCs in a turn-based environment.

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Complete Creative Freedom
- **Free-Form Building**: Start anywhere, build in any order
- **No Prescribed Workflow**: Your creativity, your way
- **Template Everything**: Save and reuse any creation
- **Mapless Design**: Focus on relationships, not geography

### 2. Deep Simulation
- **Conscious NPCs**: Characters with quantum-inspired consciousness
- **Emergent History**: Stories arise from character interactions
- **Turn-Based Control**: Thoughtful, strategic progression
- **Rich Analysis**: Comprehensive historical records

### 3. Professional Architecture
- **Clean Code**: Maintainable and extensible
- **Domain-Driven**: Clear business logic separation
- **Fully Tested**: Reliable and robust
- **Open Source**: Community-driven development
